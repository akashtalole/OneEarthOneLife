# About OneEarthOneLife

## Mission

**OneEarthOneLife** exists to remind us of a simple truth: we share one Earth, and each
of us has one life in which to care for it. The project brings together information,
tools, and community action around environmental stewardship — climate, biodiversity,
and sustainable living — so that awareness turns into everyday practice.

## Vision

A world where environmental responsibility is not a special effort but a natural part of
how individuals and communities live, build, and make decisions — because the health of
the planet and the wellbeing of every life on it are inseparable.

## What this project covers

- **Awareness** — clear, accessible information about the state of our planet and the
  choices that affect it.
- **Action** — practical guidance people can apply immediately: at home, at work, and in
  their communities.
- **Community** — a space for volunteers, contributors, and organizations working toward
  the same goal to connect and collaborate.
