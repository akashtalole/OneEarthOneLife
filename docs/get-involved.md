# Get Involved

There are many ways to support the mission of One Earth, One Life — whether you have
five minutes or five hours.

## As an individual

- **Know your footprint** — try the [Carbon Footprint Calculator](../calculator/) to see
  where your biggest impact comes from.
- **Reduce waste** — favor reusable over single-use, compost where you can, and recycle
  correctly.
- **Save water and energy** — small habits like shorter showers, efficient appliances,
  and turning off unused devices add up.
- **Choose sustainable transport** — walk, cycle, carpool, or use public transport when
  possible.
- **Support local and seasonal** — buying local produce reduces transport emissions and
  supports local ecosystems and economies.
- **Plant something** — a tree, a garden, or even a windowsill herb pot supports local
  biodiversity.
- **Share what you learn** — awareness spreads fastest through conversation.

## As a volunteer

- Join or organize local clean-up drives (beaches, parks, rivers).
- Support tree-planting or habitat-restoration efforts in your area.
- Volunteer with established environmental organizations (see [Resources](resources.md)).
- Educate others — talks, workshops, or informal conversations all help.

## As a contributor to this project

This site is open source. Contributions are welcome, including:

- Fixing typos or improving clarity in the docs.
- Adding well-sourced educational content about climate, biodiversity, or sustainability.
- Suggesting new focus areas or practical action guides.
- Improving the site itself (design, accessibility, structure).

### How to contribute

1. Fork the [repository](https://github.com/akashtalole/OneEarthOneLife).
2. Make your changes under the `docs/` folder (or the site configuration).
3. Preview locally:

   ```bash
   pip install -r requirements.txt
   mkdocs serve
   ```

4. Open a pull request describing your change.

!!! note "Keep it factual"
    When adding environmental information, please reference credible sources (see
    [Resources](resources.md) for examples of reputable organizations).
