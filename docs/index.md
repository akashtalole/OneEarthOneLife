# 🌍 One Earth, One Life

**We have one planet, and one life to live on it. Every choice we make ripples across
ecosystems, communities, and generations to come.**

OneEarthOneLife is an open initiative for anyone who wants to understand, protect, and
restore the natural world — through knowledge, small daily actions, and collective effort.

## Why it matters

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-earth: **One Earth**

    ---

    A single, finite planet supports every species alive today. Its climate, oceans,
    forests, and soil are the shared foundation of all life — there is no backup Earth.

-   :material-heart-pulse: **One Life**

    ---

    Each of us gets one life to live, and how we live it shapes the world the next
    generation inherits. Small, consistent choices compound into real change.

-   :material-tree: **Act Locally**

    ---

    Planting trees, reducing waste, conserving water, and supporting local biodiversity
    are actions anyone can take, starting today.

-   :material-account-group: **Think Collectively**

    ---

    Real environmental progress happens when individuals, communities, and organizations
    move together — sharing knowledge and holding each other accountable.

</div>

## Our focus areas

- **Climate action** — understanding climate change and reducing our collective footprint.
- **Biodiversity** — protecting habitats, wildlife, and ecosystems from further loss.
- **Sustainable living** — practical, everyday habits that reduce waste and consumption.
- **Community & education** — sharing resources so more people can take informed action.
