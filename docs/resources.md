# Resources

A starting list of credible organizations and references for learning more and taking
action. This list will grow as the project grows — suggestions are welcome via a pull
request.

## Global organizations

- [United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP)](https://www.unep.org/)
- [Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change (IPCC)](https://www.ipcc.ch/)
- [World Wildlife Fund (WWF)](https://www.worldwildlife.org/)
- [International Union for Conservation of Nature (IUCN)](https://www.iucn.org/)
- [Global Footprint Network](https://www.footprintnetwork.org/)

## Climate & sustainability

- [NASA Global Climate Change](https://climate.nasa.gov/)
- [Project Drawdown](https://drawdown.org/) — reviewed climate solutions
- [Our World in Data — Environment](https://ourworldindata.org/environment)

## Biodiversity & conservation

- [Convention on Biological Diversity](https://www.cbd.int/)
- [The Nature Conservancy](https://www.nature.org/)

## Getting hands-on

- Local municipal or community environmental groups (search for ones near you).
- National park and forestry departments, which often coordinate volunteer programs.
